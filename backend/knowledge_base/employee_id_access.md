# Employee ID & Building Access

## Overview
Every employee receives a unique Employee ID and a physical access badge for building entry. This guide covers how to obtain, replace, and troubleshoot access issues.

## Employee ID
- Your Employee ID is assigned during onboarding and sent to your personal email.
- Format: `EMP-XXXXX` (e.g., EMP-10234)
- Used for: HR portal login, IT support requests, expense claims, and internal identification.

## Building Access Badge

### New Employees
- Access badges are issued on Day 1 during the onboarding session.
- Badges provide access to your assigned building, floor, and common areas.
- Additional access zones (server rooms, labs) require manager approval.

### Lost or Damaged Badge
1. Report the loss immediately to Security at `security@company.com` or extension **2100**.
2. Your old badge will be deactivated within **1 hour** to prevent unauthorized access.
3. Request a replacement through the HR portal under **My Profile > Access Badge**.
4. Collect the replacement badge from the Security desk (Building A, Ground Floor).
5. Replacement fee: **$15** (deducted from next payroll).

### Access Issues
If your badge is not working:
1. Ensure you are scanning at the correct reader (green light = ready).
2. Check if your badge has expired — badges are valid for **12 months** and must be renewed.
3. Contact Security at extension **2100** for immediate assistance.

## Visitor Access
- Pre-register visitors at `https://security.company.com/visitors` at least **24 hours** in advance.
- Visitors must be accompanied by an employee at all times.
- Visitor badges must be returned at the end of the visit.

## Remote Employees
- Remote employees receive a digital ID through the **CompanyAuth** mobile app.
- Physical badges can be requested for occasional office visits through the HR portal.

## Contact
- Security team: `security@company.com` or extension **2100**
- HR team: `hr@company.com` or extension **3200**
