# Email Configuration Guide

## Overview
All employees are provided with a company email account (`firstname.lastname@company.com`). This guide covers email setup across different devices and common troubleshooting steps.

## Email Service
The company uses **Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online)** for email services.

## Desktop Setup (Outlook)

### Windows
1. Open **Microsoft Outlook** (pre-installed on company laptops).
2. If prompted, enter your company email address.
3. Outlook will auto-detect Exchange settings.
4. Complete MFA verification when prompted.
5. Wait for the mailbox to sync (initial sync may take 10–15 minutes).

### macOS
1. Open **Outlook for Mac** from the Applications folder.
2. Click **Add Account** and enter your company email.
3. Follow the authentication prompts and MFA verification.
4. Your mailbox will begin syncing automatically.

## Mobile Setup

### iOS
1. Go to **Settings > Mail > Accounts > Add Account**.
2. Select **Microsoft Exchange**.
3. Enter your company email and password.
4. Allow all permissions (Mail, Calendar, Contacts).

### Android
1. Open the **Outlook** app (download from Play Store if needed).
2. Tap **Add Account** and enter your company email.
3. Complete MFA verification.

## Email Signature
All employees must use the approved email signature template:
- Available at `https://itportal.company.com/email-signature`.
- Include: Full name, title, department, phone number, and company logo.

## Storage Limits
- Mailbox size limit: **50 GB**
- Maximum attachment size: **25 MB** (use OneDrive links for larger files)

## Troubleshooting
- **Cannot send/receive**: Check internet connection. Try restarting Outlook.
- **MFA issues**: Re-register your MFA device at `https://aka.ms/mfasetup`.
- **Calendar not syncing**: Remove and re-add the account.

Contact IT Support at `it-support@company.com` or extension **4500** for further assistance.
