# VPN Setup Guide

## Overview
All employees must use the company VPN when working remotely or accessing internal systems from outside the office network. The VPN ensures secure, encrypted connections to our internal infrastructure.

## Requirements
- Company-issued laptop or approved personal device
- Active employee credentials (email and password)
- VPN client software: **GlobalProtect** (version 6.x or later)

## Setup Instructions

### Windows
1. Download GlobalProtect from the IT portal at `https://itportal.company.com/vpn`.
2. Install the application and restart your computer.
3. Open GlobalProtect and enter the portal address: `vpn.company.com`.
4. Log in with your company email and password.
5. Click **Connect**. The status should change to "Connected."

### macOS
1. Download the macOS version from the IT portal.
2. Allow the system extension in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
3. Follow the same login steps as Windows.

## Troubleshooting
- **Connection timeout**: Ensure you are connected to the internet. Try switching networks.
- **Authentication failed**: Verify your credentials. If your password was recently changed, use the new password.
- **Portal unreachable**: Contact IT Support at `it-support@company.com` or extension **4500**.

## Policies
- VPN must remain connected during all remote work hours.
- Do not share VPN credentials with anyone.
- Report any VPN issues within 24 hours to IT Support.
