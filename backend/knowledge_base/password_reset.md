# Password Reset Policy

## Overview
Employee passwords must meet security requirements and be changed regularly. This guide covers the password reset process and company password policies.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum **12 characters**
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character
- Cannot reuse the last **5 passwords**
- Passwords expire every **90 days**

## Self-Service Password Reset

### Via IT Portal
1. Navigate to `https://itportal.company.com/password-reset`.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Complete the multi-factor authentication (MFA) verification.
4. Set your new password following the requirements above.
5. Log out of all devices and log back in with the new password.

### Via Mobile App
1. Open the **CompanyAuth** app on your phone.
2. Tap "Reset Password."
3. Verify your identity using biometrics or a one-time code.
4. Enter and confirm your new password.

## Account Lockout
- After **5 consecutive failed login attempts**, your account will be locked for **30 minutes**.
- If locked out, wait 30 minutes or contact IT Support for an immediate unlock.

## Emergency Access
If you are unable to reset your password through self-service:
1. Contact IT Support at `it-support@company.com`.
2. Call the IT helpdesk at extension **4500** (available 8 AM–6 PM).
3. Provide your employee ID for identity verification.

## Security Reminders
- Never share your password with colleagues or supervisors.
- Do not write down passwords or store them in plain text files.
- Use the company-approved password manager (**1Password Business**) for storing credentials.
