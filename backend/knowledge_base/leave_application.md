# Leave Application Process

## Overview
Employees can apply for various types of leave through the HR portal. All leave requests must be submitted at least **3 business days** in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.

## Leave Types and Entitlements

| Leave Type       | Annual Entitlement | Carry Forward |
|------------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Annual Leave     | 20 days            | Up to 5 days  |
| Sick Leave       | 12 days            | No            |
| Personal Leave   | 5 days             | No            |
| Parental Leave   | 16 weeks (paid)    | No            |
| Bereavement      | 5 days             | No            |

## How to Apply

1. Log in to the HR portal at `https://hr.company.com`.
2. Navigate to **My Leave > Apply for Leave**.
3. Select the leave type and specify the dates.
4. Add a reason or notes (required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days).
5. Submit the request. Your manager will be notified automatically.

## Approval Process
- **Manager approval**: Required for all leave requests within **2 business days**.
- **HR approval**: Required for parental leave and extended sick leave (5+ days).
- If no response within 2 business days, the request is auto-escalated to the department head.

## Sick Leave Documentation
- Sick leave of **3 or more consecutive days** requires a medical certificate.
- Upload the certificate to the HR portal within **5 business days** of returning.

## Cancellation
- Approved leave can be cancelled up to **1 business day** before the start date.
- Contact HR at `hr@company.com` for last-minute changes.

## Questions
For leave-related queries, contact the HR team at `hr@company.com` or extension **3200**.
