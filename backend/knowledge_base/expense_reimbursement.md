# Expense Reimbursement Guide

## Overview
Employees can submit expense reimbursement claims for approved business-related expenses. All claims must comply with the company expense policy.

## Eligible Expenses
- **Travel**: Flights, trains, taxis, ride-shares, hotel accommodation
- **Meals**: Client meetings, team events (max **$50 per person per meal**)
- **Office Supplies**: Pre-approved purchases only
- **Professional Development**: Conference tickets, training courses (requires manager pre-approval)
- **Software/Tools**: Only if not available through IT procurement

## Submission Process

1. Log in to the Finance portal at `https://finance.company.com`.
2. Navigate to **Expenses > Submit New Claim**.
3. Fill in the expense details:
   - Date of expense
   - Category (travel, meals, supplies, etc.)
   - Amount and currency
   - Business justification
4. Upload receipt images (JPEG, PNG, or PDF). **Receipts are mandatory** for expenses over $25.
5. Submit the claim for approval.

## Approval Workflow
- **Under $200**: Manager approval only.
- **$200–$1,000**: Manager + Finance team approval.
- **Over $1,000**: Manager + Finance Director approval.

## Reimbursement Timeline
- Approved claims are processed within **10 business days**.
- Reimbursements are paid via direct deposit to the bank account on file.
- Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date.

## Rejected Claims
If your claim is rejected:
1. Review the rejection reason in the Finance portal.
2. Correct the issue and resubmit with additional documentation.
3. Contact Finance at `finance@company.com` if you need clarification.

## Policy Notes
- Personal expenses are never reimbursable.
- Alcohol is only reimbursable during approved client entertainment events.
- Keep original receipts for at least **12 months** in case of audit.
