# Laptop Request Process

## Overview
All employees are provided with a company-issued laptop. This guide covers how to request a new laptop, replacement, or upgrade.

## New Employee Laptops
- New hires receive their laptop on their first day, pre-configured by IT.
- Standard configuration: **Dell Latitude 5550** or **MacBook Pro 14"** (based on department policy).
- Developers and designers may request upgraded specifications during onboarding.

## Requesting a Replacement or Upgrade

### Replacement (Damaged/Defective)
1. Report the issue to IT Support at `it-support@company.com` with:
   - Asset tag number (found on the bottom of the laptop)
   - Description of the issue
   - Photos of physical damage (if applicable)
2. IT will assess whether the laptop can be repaired or needs replacement.
3. Replacement laptops are typically available within **3–5 business days**.

### Upgrade Request
1. Submit an upgrade request through the IT portal at `https://itportal.company.com/hardware`.
2. Select your desired configuration and provide a business justification.
3. Approval required from your **manager** and **IT Director**.
4. Upgraded laptops are provisioned within **7–10 business days**.

## Available Configurations

| Role        | Standard Option        | Upgrade Option            |
|-------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| General     | Dell Latitude 5550     | Dell Latitude 7450        |
| Developer   | MacBook Pro 14" M3     | MacBook Pro 16" M3 Max    |
| Designer    | MacBook Pro 14" M3     | MacBook Pro 16" M3 Max    |
| Executive   | MacBook Pro 16" M3 Pro | Custom configuration      |

## Return Policy
- When leaving the company, laptops must be returned to IT on the last working day.
- All data will be securely wiped per company policy.
- Failure to return equipment may result in payroll deductions.

## Support
For hardware issues, contact IT Support at extension **4500** or `it-support@company.com`.
